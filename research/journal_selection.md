# Journal Selection (Oxford University Press)

**Author:** Rajveersinh Vishal Pardeshi (Independent Researcher)
**Date:** 2026-10-17
**Contribution:** Sharp two-point MGF concentration bound; explicit improvement over Hoeffding; reproducible numerical verification. Classified as incremental/expository refinement of classical material (Hoeffding 1963).

## Primary recommendation: The Computer Journal (OUP)

**Why scope fits:**
- The Computer Journal serves "all branches of the academic computer science community" and has four sections. Section A = "Computer Science Theory, Methods and Tools," with primary focus on "theory, software and system architectures that are central to computer science," covering "design and analysis of algorithms" and "mathematics of computing (primarily discrete mathematics)."
- Concentration inequalities are a core tool in randomized algorithms, streaming, sketching, and machine-learning theory, so the paper belongs naturally in Section A (theory/methods), with a computational verification component.
- Readership: computer science theory and methods researchers — the right audience for a refined concentration bound with code.

**Source:** Official OUP "About | The Computer Journal" page (sections A–D), accessed via Oxford Academic search result.

**Official author instructions:** https://academic.oup.com/comjnl/pages/General_Instructions and https://academic.oup.com/comjnl/pages/Manuscript_Preparation_Submission

**Publishing model / fees (verified from official OUP pages):**
- The Computer Journal is a **hybrid open access** journal.
- **Standard licence route (subscription): no mandatory author publication fee.** There is no submission fee and no mandatory publication charge. Open access is optional: "If you wish to publish your article open access, you should select your choice of open access licence... You will need to pay an open access charge to publish under an open access licence." The standard (non-OA) licence requires no APC.
- **Optional open-access charge:** applies only if the author elects OA (CC-BY / CC-BY-NC / CC-BY-NC-ND). Not mandatory.
- **Other possible charges:** "Extensive changes to the text of the paper may be charged to the author" (proof-stage); authors grant an exclusive licence to BCS on acceptance. Print copies available at discount. No page or colour charge identified in the snippets reviewed.
- **Waivers/institutional agreements:** not assumed to apply (author is Independent Researcher, no institutional agreement). The no-mandatory-fee route is the standard subscription licence, which does not depend on a waiver.

**No-mandatory-author-fee route:** Publish under the standard (subscription) licence; do not elect open access. No APC is payable. This satisfies the requirement. Verified via official OUP author-instructions snippets (General Instructions + Manuscript Preparation + Publish with Us), 2026-10-17.

**Preprint / public-repository policy (OUP Author Self-Archiving Policy, official):**
- The Author's Original Version (AOV) = the un-refereed preprint as submitted. "Authors may make their AOV available anywhere at any time," including personal/institutional/non-commercial/commercial websites and repositories and social media. Upon acceptance, acknowledge "accepted for publication in [Journal Title] Published by Oxford University Press" and add the DOI/link to the Version of Record after publication.
- Therefore, posting the **preprint** manuscript (main.tex / PDF) on a **public GitHub repository before submission is permitted** by OUP policy. The public repository may contain the paper source as a preprint.
- The Accepted Manuscript (post-print) and Version of Record are subject to embargo/exclusive-licence rules (AM after an embargo period; VoR upon publication with credit line). Those would only apply if/when the paper is accepted. No submission has occurred.

**Verification notes / unresolved ambiguity:**
- The exact APC dollar amount for optional OA was not captured (OUP pages 403'd on direct fetch; search snippets confirm an optional charge exists but not the figure). This does not affect the no-mandatory-fee route.
- "Extensive changes to the text of the paper may be charged to the author" — ambiguous whether/when it applies; it is a proof-stage production charge, not a mandatory publication fee. Not assumed.
- The Computer Journal's journal-specific embargo period (for the Accepted Manuscript) was not individually confirmed; the general OUP list is typical (~12 months) but journal-specific. Marked as unresolved; only relevant post-acceptance.

**Conclusion:** The Computer Journal offers a credible no-mandatory-author-fee route (standard subscription licence), a reasonable scope fit (Section A theory/algorithms), and a preprint policy compatible with a public GitHub repository. Recommended as the primary target, with acceptance prospects honestly assessed as uncertain-to-low due to incremental novelty.

## Alternative OUP candidates (not primary, listed for completeness)

1. **The Quarterly Journal of Mathematics (OUP)** — pure/applied mathematics. Scope: broad; a concentration inequality might fit analysis/probability-adjacent content but is not a clear match. Fee model: hybrid with optional OA (institutional read-and-publish deals noted); standard route no mandatory APC. Less certain scope fit than The Computer Journal.

2. **Biometrika (OUP)** — leading theoretical statistics journal. Focus is statistical methodology with substantive statistical content; a pure concentration-inequality refinement may be too theoretical/probabilistic and too incremental for Biometrika's standards. Hybrid; optional OA with APC; standard route no mandatory APC.

No alternative is asserted as a qualifying choice; each would need independent fee and scope verification before submission.
