# Limitations

**Author:** Rajveersinh Vishal Pardeshi (Independent Researcher)
**Date:** 2026-10-17

## Scientific limitations
1. **Classical roots.** The two-point MGF bound (Hoeffding 1963), the Chernoff/Cramér minimization (Chernoff 1952), and the Bernoulli worst-case MGF fact are established. The contribution is an incremental/expository refinement (explicit assembled bound, quantified Hoeffding gap, verification), not a new fundamental inequality.
2. **No exact prefactor.** The bound is exponential in the rate but lacks the polynomial prefactor of sharp large-deviation asymptotics; conservative relative to exact binomial tails (quantified in Table 2).
3. **Numerical minimization in the general case.** Theorem 1 is an infimum over λ; a closed form is available only in special cases (e.g., homogeneous Bernoulli, Corollary 1).
4. **Independence only.** Full independence is assumed; dependent/martingale settings are out of scope.
5. **Synthetic validation.** Numerical validation uses Bernoulli variables (the two-point worst case) with fixed seeds; no real-world dataset is used. This is appropriate for verifying an inequality but is not an empirical study.

## Production limitations
6. **Manuscript not compiled.** No LaTeX distribution is available in this environment, so main.tex has not been compiled to PDF. A final compile-and-verify step remains before submission. (Documented in paper/README.md.)
7. **Not submitted.** The manuscript has not been submitted to any journal; no external peer review has occurred; no acceptance is claimed.

## Ethical / data limitations
8. No human or animal subjects; no personal data; no private or restricted datasets. All data are synthetic and generated by the provided code. No ethics approval was sought or is needed. No funding was received. No conflicts of interest.

## What this means
The work should be read as a verified, reproducible refinement of a classical concentration bound, with all limitations disclosed. It is not a claim of state-of-the-art performance or a novel fundamental theorem.
